# The lines of readable/src that no test and no scenario runs, and why.
# tests/line_coverage.py --write writes this file and keeps each reason;
# `make line-coverage` measures again and holds the tree to it.
#
# Measured with Ubuntu clang version 18.1.3 (1ubuntu1).
# 422 files hold lines of C to run (406 of the 474 .c files under src/,
# and 16 headers); 59974 such lines; 1558 of them unrun
# (2.60%), in 651 stretches of 216 files.
# Built and never run: x86-flight-cm-core, x86-flight-lm-core, x86-link-check.
#
# These are lines of C only. Interpretive code is tables of words, which
# hold no line to run: 811 interpretive programs in 199 files of src/
# (144 of them files with C lines that are counted, the rest among the
# files not counted, below). Which of their words the interpreter ran,
# this measure does not see.
#
# By reason:
#   impossible: 221 lines in 97 stretches
#   never: 76 lines in 33 stretches
#   not-modelled: 111 lines in 83 stretches
#   open: 383 lines in 150 stretches
#   refusal: 767 lines in 288 stretches
#
# Run, and compared with nothing: 84 lines of 81 functions ran
# and refuse (not_modelled() first on the line). A case or a scenario
# brought the routine there and its binding refused the state: the tests
# hold only that a refusal leaves the state as it found it. They are
# listed at the end, by function.
#
# A reason in brackets is one of these:

[impossible] No state brings the routine to the line: the routine's own
    lines, or its file's, rule the outcome out (what they leave in the word
    the branch tests), and the reason says how. The line follows the
    listing's instructions for the outcome. An argument, not a run: nothing
    compares the line.
[never] No program brings the state to the line, though the routine's own
    lines do not rule it out: the reason rests on what the rest of the
    program stores there or on what the assembler makes, and says what, and
    what was looked at. The comment at the line says never. The line follows
    the listing's instructions for the state; where the reason says there is
    no faithful run, nothing compares the line.
[no-code] Of a source: it holds no line of C to run, only tables, names and
    words. Most are interpretive programs, which are tables of words: the
    interpreter's lines run them, and this measure does not see which of
    their words ran (nor in the many sources that hold such tables beside C
    lines of their own; the head counts both). The build compiles the source
    (the check holds it to that), and a function added to it would be counted
    from then on.
[not-built] Of a file: no target of the build compiles it, or no source the
    build compiles reads it, and the reason says why it stands in src/.
[not-modelled] The stretch is, or ends in, not_modelled(): the source marks
    the outcome as one that cannot come there (a CCS outcome its word never
    has, a value outside a switch's table). A routine that met it would stop
    and its binding refuse the state. One that a case or a scenario does
    bring a routine to is run, and so is in no stretch here, but nothing was
    compared at it: those are listed at the end.
[open] Not looked at yet: owed a case that runs it, or the reason none can.
    Item 3 of the validation plan is not done while a stretch says this.
[refusal] Part of how a binding refuses a state its routines do not model: a
    hook that only refuses (a channel or a fixed-memory address the file's
    routines do not use), the jump out (leave), a key its table does not
    have, or the lines that put the state and the io record back and answer
    out of scope. It runs only if a routine of the file is given such a
    state. No case's state and no scenario does.
