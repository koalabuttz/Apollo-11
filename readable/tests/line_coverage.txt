#   refusal: 777 lines in 290 stretches
#
# A reason in brackets is one of these:

[impossible] No state brings the routine to the line: what the lines before
    it leave in the word the branch tests rules the outcome out, and the
    reason says how. The line is what the AGC would do there.
[never] The comment at the line says the outcome never comes, and why. The
    line is what the AGC would do if it did.
[not-modelled] The line is, or leads only to, not_modelled(): the source
    marks the outcome as one that cannot come there (a CCS outcome its word
    never has, a value outside a switch's table). A routine that met it would
    stop and its binding refuse the state.
[open] Not looked at yet: owed a case that runs it, or the reason none can.
    Item 3 of the validation plan is not done while a stretch says this.
[refusal] Part of how a binding refuses a state its routines do not model: a
    hook that only refuses (a channel or a fixed-memory address the file's
    routines do not use), the jump out (leave), a key its table does not
    have, or the lines that put the state and the io record back and answer
    out of scope. It runs only if a routine of the file is given such a
    state. No case's state and no scenario does.
