# Original two-integer data display

`dsky_two_integer` translates source 2INTOUT, LM40,2732 and CM40,2636, with five explicit stages BLANK/SIGN/FIRST/SECOND/RETURN. It blanks the selected data row, writes its positive sign, formats the cached first positive integer in D1/D2, reads the second integer fresh through READLO and formats it in D4/D5, retaining the blank D3. The terminal original POSTJUMP reaches DSPDCEND+2 at41,2570 before the descending row loop executes.

Positive SP integers0..99, DECOUNT0..2, ordinary RAM, coherent bank40/allEB, selecting SUPER100/120/140/160, legal IRQ, nonzero relay words and caller-owned producer exclusion are required. FIRST requires its actual RXD1 count14/9/4. SECOND accepts normal MIXBR1 or mixed MIXBR2 ordinary fresh pairs0100..3776 without bank crossing and a positive low word0..99; pairs whose low word is TEM4 are excluded because the source saves its caller there before reading. Each stage retains source registers, physical scratch, bank changes, fresh-read ordering and signed-zero arithmetic. Invalid declared control contexts leave image/IO unchanged. No decoder, floating point or heap is used.

The immutable fiveblank, sign-output, READLO and Pinball arithmetic kernels are reused. The source DSPDECVN data-position arithmetic is separately composed here: existing published verb/noun two-digit helpers only qualify odd positions, whereas source 2INTOUT first calls the even data positions14/4. Those older modules remain unchanged.
