# Translate Luminary099 P34-35_P74-75

Lane E (targeting). Milestone `lm-p34-p35`. How the routines are made and checked: `docs/INTERP-COMPILE.md`.

`Luminary099/P34-35_P74-75.agc` (`src/lm_p34_p35.c`) is native C in full. All 1,056 words, across banks 04, 06, 10, 11, 23 and 35, are compiled by `tools/interp_compile.py` into 509 routines of the control-transfer ABI. The spec is `lm-p34-p35` in `tools/interp_specs.py`, Luminary's twin of `cm-p34-p35`. Luminary's VNPOOH doesn't switch FBANK itself, so unlike Comanche's it compiles too.

This replaces the file's earlier hand-written pieces. LM-PERIAPO, LM-INITVEL, LM-MIDGIM, LM-VN1645, LM-S3435 and LM-S3435B stay in the tree and in the coverage record. LM-P34-TRANSFER, LM-S3435C, LM-P34P74 and LM-P35 held the same routine addresses as this file and were retired, with their coverage entries.

## Spec

- RTB LOADTIME, DPMODE and TPMODE run native bodies (`src/rtb_angles.c`, `src/rtb_modes.c`); SGNAGREE ends the routine.
- RTX1 and RTX2 (E5,1776-1777) are the earth or moon pair (-2, 0 or -10, 2), so INITVELX's `DLOAD* MUTABLE -2,1` reads one of MUTABLE's two entries. SETMUER's X1 is the same.

## Evidence

Hashes: `evidence/lm-p34-p35/SHA256.json`. Bulk files are assets of the `evidence-lm-p34-p35` release.

- **Source map.** `lm-p34-p35-source-map.json` lists every word with the routine that compiles it.
- **Independent assembly comparison.** `make lm-p34-p35-differential` runs 512 states per routine (260,608 cases). All equal yaAGC in Z, all 2,048 erasable words and the interrupt-enable state. Every 512th case is frozen in `tests/lm_p34_p35_vectors.h`.
- **Host and sanitizer results.** `make test-lm-p34-p35 sanitize-lm-p34-p35` runs the frozen cases (`lm-p34-p35-host.log`).
- **C64 execution results.** `make vice-lm-p34-p35` runs 48 diagnostics with 597 cases, 25 of them truncated (`lm-p34-p35-vice.log`). Every routine runs on the C64; none is host only.
