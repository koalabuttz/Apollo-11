Every instruction of Comanche055 FRESH_START_AND_RESTART in bank 05 and fixed-fixed (V36 fresh start, hardware and software restart with the phase-table check and group restarts, the optics, coarse-alignment and TVC restarts, ENEMA, the KLEAN routines, the STARTSUB family, T5IDLOC and GOTOPOOH) runs as routines of the control-transfer ABI. There are 17 entries, including the return after every TC to code outside the file, and the file's constants and T5IDLER table are compiled from the source listing. 4,096 cases from every entry equal the original Comanche055 program in the interrupt-enable state, SUPERBNK, channels 5, 6, 11, 12, 13, 14 and 33, the trap latches and all 2,048 erasable words. That is 8,965 strict and sanitizer checks, with all 17 entries and all 13 kinds of exit reached; together the cases execute every instruction but STARTSIM, and 259 C64 rows chosen by native branch outcome (`tools/a2_c64_rows.py`: every 16th case plus a greedy cover of the gcov branch arcs of every linked source; the freeze fails unless the rows take all 503 arcs the host cases take) match in VICE. A quarter of the cases have the AGC warning persisting (its channel 33 latch sets again after GOPROG's reset), so the restart-loop fresh start with the warning still on (05,2545) is compared. V37/POOH (bank 04), VAC5STOR (bank 20) and bank 13 are later milestones. Lane A2 (restart, telemetry and support). See `docs/CM-FRESH-START.md`.
