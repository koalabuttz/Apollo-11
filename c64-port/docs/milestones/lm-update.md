Every instruction of Luminary099 UPDATE_PROGRAM (P27: the V70-V73 uplink verbs, data collection and verification, restart-protected stores into erasable memory and the TIME2/TIME1 adjustment) runs as routines of the control-transfer ABI. There are 35 entries: the verbs, the POSTJUMP targets, the job, its post-EXIT code, two restart points and the return after every TC out of the file, plus UPJOB's two interpretive words (CALL INTSTALL, UPWAKE EXIT) registered at NEWOPS as the CHANG2 resume LOCs, with NEWJOB positive in a fifth of their cases. The file's own BANKCALL/POSTJUMP CADRs and constants are compiled from the source listing. 4,096 cases from every entry equal the original Luminary099 program in the interrupt-enable state, SUPERBNK, channel 11 and all 2,048 erasable words. They include even V72 counts, one-word V71 loads at a bank's first word, V71 loads past a bank's end and EB0 targets, and together they execute every instruction of the file (12,666 strict and sanitizer checks, all 35 entries, both interpretive entries and all 14 exits reached). 261 C64 rows chosen by native branch outcome (`tools/a2_c64_rows.py`: every 16th case plus a greedy cover of the gcov branch arcs of every linked source; the freeze fails unless the rows take all 446 arcs the host cases take) match in VICE. Lane A2 (restart, telemetry and support). See `docs/LM-UPDATE.md`.
