Every instruction of AGC_BLOCK_TWO_SELF-CHECK in both missions runs as a native routine of the control-transfer ABI, from one shared body (`src/self_check_body.inc`) that each mission builds with its own addresses and constants. That includes the self-check idle loop SMODECHK with its options, -1CHK, ERRORS/PRERRORS with the ERESTORE repair, ERASCHK, CNTRCHK, CYCLSHFT, and ROPECHK/STSHOSUM with FXADRS, ADSUM, ADRSCHK, NXTBNK and BNKCHK. FXADRS and SUPDACAL read the unmodified rope through a rope image (`include/rope_image.h`: the assembled rope on the host, a 2 MB REU image on the C64) that only this file may use. 4,096 random-state cases per mission from all 11 entries, and 43 whole-rope cases (a full ROPECHK of all 36 banks, the NEWJOB branches, STSHOSUM, and SHOWSUM through every display), equal the original Luminary099 and Comanche055 programs in the interrupt-enable state, SUPERBNK and all 2,048 erasable words. Every instruction of the file is executed but two +0 branch outcomes no state can reach. The C64 rows (74 + 6 per mission, chosen by outcome, one PRG per mission with the REU image) match in VICE.
