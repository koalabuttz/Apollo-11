# Translate Comanche P40-P47

Lane G (CM flight). Milestone `cm-p40-p47`. How the routines are made and checked: `docs/INTERP-COMPILE.md` (whole-file basic code: `tools/basic_compile.py`).

`Comanche055/P40-P47.agc` (`src/cm_p40_p47.c`) is native C in full: The thrusting programs: P40 and P41 (SPS and RCS burns) with the TIG countdown tasks, the engine-on and engine-off sequences, the gimbal-drive test, S40.1 to S40.13 (steering, cross-product steering and the AVG ignition entries), DAPFIG and P47, basic and interpretive. Each routine runs the file from its entry, or from where it resumes, to the point where control leaves the file. Every original word is one call of the basic-instruction primitives (`src/agc_image_ops.c`) under a label named for its address, with TS overflow skips, CCS four-way skips and INDEX on the whole word kept; computed transfers (TC Q, indexed TCF) go through one dispatch on the target address.

## Flight states (2026-10-18)

NBRCYCLS (E6,1742), held under every EBANK by the spec's `cells`, is -1 in about an eighth of the cases, as P40SXTY and every S40.9 pass leave it. ## Domain

Canonical 2048-word image (every word random, the counters, `TIME1`-`TIME6`, the CDUs and the save registers at 010-061 included) under the routine's `EBANK=`, FB of the routine's bank, any A, L and Q unless stated, and any values of the other erasables. `NEWJOB` is +0 or, in a quarter of the cases, positive: a job waiting, so any `DANZIG` goes to `CHANG2`, where the routine stops; every interpretive word that can follow a `DANZIG` is a `CHANG2` resume key of its own. Each routine runs under the EBANK= the listing has in effect at its first word. Interpretive routines are entered at NEWOPS with FIXLOC a VAC area; P47BODY's interpretive block (P47BODY+1, +3, +4 and +5), which runs in the NOVAC job TIGON starts, also with FIXLOC 0110 (NOVAC's LOC(MPAC+6) - LOC(QPRET)) in a quarter of its cases (spec key `novac`). DAPFIG (bank 42) runs under FB 32 with the superbank bit, as the flight rope addresses it. Under every EBANK (V97ETASK reaches E6 through E6SETTER), CNTR (E6,1447) holds +0, 1, -0 or -1, NBRCYCLS (E6,1742) is short of 037777 (its +1 never overflows into the OCT after TC PHASCHNG), and CSMMASS (E6,1474) stays within the CSM's mass, so V97TTASK's AD 3MDOT never overflows into the PHASCHNG operand. OUTPUT's return after FIXDELAY (TEMPR60) is one of GIMDTEST's three TC OUTPUT returns. P41/P40 returns to the call +1 or, after INCR Q, +2. With PHASE5 negative, PRECHECK's CCS runs the CADR PREREAD word as an instruction, on the AGC and in the port.

## Evidence

Hashes: `evidence/cm-p40-p47/SHA256.json`; all files are in the tree.

- **Source map.** `cm-p40-p47-source-map.json` lists all 1667 words of the file with the routine that compiles each (or why a constant is never run as code); 0 are untranslated.
- **Independent assembly comparison.** `make cm-p40-p47-differential` runs 256 states per routine (126,976 cases) through the original Comanche055 rope in yaAGC (pinned yaAGC with hardware DV and without the IMU/optics device block (tools/prepare_flight_device_engine.py), its ScalerCounter held at 0 (TIME1-TIME6 and the timer interrupts do not advance during a case)) and the native routines; all are equal in Z, 2048 erasable words (A, L 16-bit) and the interrupt-enable state, the I/O channels 05, 11, 12, 14, 31 and the 31A/31B/32 trap flags. The cases and the stops reached per routine are in `cm-p40-p47-proof.json` (`cm-p40-p47-differential.log` is the run). One case in 128 is frozen in `tests/cm_p40_p47_vectors.h`.
- **Host and sanitizer results.** `make test-cm-p40-p47 sanitize-cm-p40-p47` runs the 992 frozen cases (`cm-p40-p47-host.log`).
- **C64 execution results.** `make vice-cm-p40-p47` runs 51 diagnostic programs with 646 cases (`cm-p40-p47-vice.log`, which starts with each PRG's SHA-256). The cases are chosen so the C64 runs take every control-flow edge (both outcomes of each branch) of every linked source that the 126,976 differential cases take: 4662 of 4662 edges and comparison outcomes (clang -O0 trace-pc-guard,trace-cmp) and 820 of 820 compiled labels. Checked again with gcov -b over gcc -O0 `--coverage` builds of the same sources: the C64 cases take 2738 of the 2738 branch outcomes all the cases take (`c64_gcov_branches` in the proof).

## Flight build

The spec sets `trusted_image`. In flight builds (`RK_FIXED_IMAGE`), the entry checks skip their scan of the whole erasable image, which costs about 121,000 cycles a routine on the C64. Host tests and C64 diagnostics keep the scan.
