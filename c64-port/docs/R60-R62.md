# Translate R60,R62

Lane D1b (alignment and maneuvers). Milestone `r60-r62`. How the routines are made and checked: `docs/INTERP-COMPILE.md`.

`Luminary099/R60_62.agc` (`src/lm_r60_r62.c`) and `Comanche055/R60_62.agc` (`src/cm_r60_r62.c`) are native C except for the words that read I/O channels. The assembly listing names the file `R60,R62.agc`. R60 is the attitude maneuver routine (the V50 N18 display, the KALCMANU call and its restarts), R62 the crew-defined maneuver, and VECPOINT/BALLANGS the attitude computations they share.

## Scope

| Luminary099 | Source | Leaves at |
|---|---|---|
| 26,2123 R60LEM to 26,2241 | R60, TOBALL and TOBALLA, STARTMNV, ENDMANUV, RELINUS and CHKLINUS restarts | MAKECADR, BANKCALL, PHASCHNG, LINUS, GOTOPOOH, ENDOFJOB |
| 26,2266 BALLANGS to 26,2372 PICKX | FDAI ball angles, ROT180, XROT | the caller, RTB V1STO2S |
| 27,2032 VECPNT1 to 27,2170 PICKAXIS | VECPOINT: the desired attitude from a pointing vector | the caller |
| 23,2103 R62FLASH to 23,2115 | R62's display and KALCMANU request | BANKCALL, GOTOV56 |

| Comanche055 | Source | Leaves at |
|---|---|---|
| 27,2000 R60CSM to 27,2113 | R60, TOBALL, STARTMNV, ENDMANUV, RELINUS | as Luminary |
| 27,2126 VECPOINT to 27,2310 PICKX | VECPOINT, ROT180, XROT | the caller |
| 27,2330 R62DISP to 27,2342 | R62 | BANKCALL, GOTOV56 |

Not translated: Luminary `G+N,AUTO` and `ISITAUTO` (`READ CHAN30`, `READ CHAN31`) and Comanche `TOBALLC` (`RXOR CHAN31`). They wait for an I/O channel model in the control-transfer ABI (`agc_hardware` holds channel 11 only); the compiled routines stop with `EXEC_OUT_OF_SCOPE` at those words, and the source map records them as untranslated with that reason.

Destinations outside the file (the display interface, Executive, restart and flag routines, KALCMANU through BANKCALL) stop with `EXEC_UNMAPPED` until their files have routines. Returns from displays and restarts land on words nothing in the file branches to; the compiler starts a routine at each of them.

## Evidence

Hashes: `evidence/r60-r62/SHA256.json`. Bulk files are assets of the `evidence-r60-r62` release.

- **Source map.** `r60-r62-source-map.json` lists every emitted word with the routine that compiles it or the reason it is not compiled. Luminary has 296 words (3 untranslated), Comanche 227 (2 untranslated).
- **Independent assembly comparison.** `make r60-r62-differential`: 512 states per routine and program (59,392 cases) equal yaAGC in Z, all 2,048 erasable words and the interrupt-enable state. BUF2 holds a fixed-memory return, as CHKLINUS's restart point assumes. The 3,580 cases that reach a channel read are listed in `r60-r62-proof.json` as `waiting_for_io_channels` and are not compared.
- **Host and sanitizer results.** `make test-r60-r62 sanitize-r60-r62` runs the 872 frozen cases (every 64th).
- **C64 execution results.** `make vice-r60-r62` runs eight diagnostics (four per program) with 220 cases.
