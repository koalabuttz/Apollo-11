# Translate R60,R62

Lane D1b (alignment and maneuvers); channel reads and refreeze by Lane G (CM flight). Milestone `r60-r62`. How the routines are made and checked: `docs/INTERP-COMPILE.md`.

`Luminary099/R60_62.agc` (`src/lm_r60_r62.c`) and `Comanche055/R60_62.agc` (`src/cm_r60_r62.c`) are native C, every word. The assembly listing names the file `R60,R62.agc`. R60 is the attitude maneuver routine (the V50 N18 display, the KALCMANU call and its restarts), R62 the crew-defined maneuver, and VECPOINT/BALLANGS the attitude computations they share.

## Scope

| Luminary099 | Source | Leaves at |
|---|---|---|
| 26,2123 R60LEM to 26,2241 | R60, TOBALL and TOBALLA, STARTMNV, ENDMANUV, RELINUS and CHKLINUS restarts | MAKECADR, BANKCALL, PHASCHNG, LINUS, GOTOPOOH, ENDOFJOB |
| 26,2266 BALLANGS to 26,2372 PICKX | FDAI ball angles, ROT180, XROT | the caller, RTB V1STO2S |
| 27,2032 VECPNT1 to 27,2170 PICKAXIS | VECPOINT: the desired attitude from a pointing vector | the caller |
| 23,2103 R62FLASH to 23,2115 | R62's display and KALCMANU request | BANKCALL, GOTOV56 |

| Comanche055 | Source | Leaves at |
|---|---|---|
| 27,2000 R60CSM to 27,2113 | R60, TOBALL, STARTMNV, ENDMANUV, RELINUS | as Luminary |
| 27,2126 VECPOINT to 27,2310 PICKX | VECPOINT, ROT180, XROT | the caller |
| 27,2330 R62DISP to 27,2342 | R62 | BANKCALL, GOTOV56 |

Channel reads: Luminary `G+N,AUTO` and `ISITAUTO` (`READ CHAN30`, `READ CHAN31`) and Comanche `TOBALLC` (`RXOR CHAN31`) read the run's latched input channels (`channel_inputs`, `include/agc_input_channels.h`): A is overflow-corrected and sign-extended as yaAGC does, and the oracle latches the same channel values for each case. Channels 30 and 31 take any value in every case.

Destinations outside the file (the display interface, Executive, restart and flag routines, KALCMANU through BANKCALL) stop with `EXEC_UNMAPPED` until their files have routines. Returns from displays and restarts land on words nothing in the file branches to; the compiler starts a routine at each of them.

## Evidence

Hashes: `evidence/r60-r62/SHA256.json`. Bulk files are assets of the `evidence-r60-r62` release.

- **Source map.** `r60-r62-source-map.json` lists every emitted word with the routine that compiles it or the reason it is not compiled. Luminary has 296 words and Comanche 227; none is untranslated.
- **Independent assembly comparison.** `make r60-r62-differential`: 512 states per routine and program (100,352 cases) equal yaAGC in Z, all 2,048 erasable words and the interrupt-enable state. A quarter of the cases put edge values into MPAC and every operand the file names: +0 and -0, one LSB, 1/2, 1/4, just below 1, half-unit axis vectors (with a matrix's rows as a signed permutation, so identity and 90° rotations occur), unit vectors in a coordinate plane, and tiny vectors. In those cases the CDUs sit at 0, 45, 90, 180 or 270°. TIME1, TIME2 and the CDUs vary in every case. BUF2 holds a fixed-memory return, as CHKLINUS's restart point assumes.
- **Resume points.** Resume points: a DANZIG that finds NEWJOB positive goes to CHANG2 and the job resumes later at the next word, so with `resume_keys` every word after a DANZIG is a routine of its own (the routine counts include them). A quarter of the cases at those routines have NEWJOB positive, and the run stops at CHANG2 as the original does. Each resume point starts from the MODE, PUSHLOC and index registers the code before it leaves there (per-point `states`). Runs may take up to 1,000 routines (`max_steps`).
- **Host and sanitizer results.** `make test-r60-r62 sanitize-r60-r62` runs the 836 frozen cases (every 128th plus a greedy cover of every branch outcome any case reaches).
- **C64 execution results.** `make vice-r60-r62` runs nine diagnostics with 260 cases, chosen so all 1,703 branch outcomes the host cases reach are reached on the C64 too (`r60-r62-c64-outcomes.json`); the log records each PRG's sha256.
