# Translate Comanche P61-P67

Lane G (CM flight). Milestone `cm-p61-p67`. How the routines are made and checked: `docs/INTERP-COMPILE.md` (whole-file basic code: `tools/basic_compile.py`).

`Comanche055/P61-P67.agc` (`src/cm_p61_p67.c`) is native C in full: The entry programs: P61 and P62 (entry preparation, CM/SM separation, with the S61.1 IMU orientation check), P63 (entry initialisation), P64 to P67 (post-0.05 G, up control, ballistic and final phases) with their displays and restart points, basic and interpretive. Each routine runs the file from its entry, or from where it resumes, to the point where control leaves the file. Every original word is one call of the basic-instruction primitives (`src/agc_image_ops.c`) under a label named for its address, with TS overflow skips, CCS four-way skips and INDEX on the whole word kept; computed transfers (TC Q, indexed TCF) go through one dispatch on the target address.

## Domain

Canonical 2048-word image (every word random, the counters, `TIME1`-`TIME6`, the CDUs and the save registers at 010-061 included) under the routine's `EBANK=`, FB of the routine's bank, any A, L and Q unless stated, and any values of the other erasables. `NEWJOB` is +0 or, in a quarter of the cases, positive: a job waiting, so any `DANZIG` goes to `CHANG2`, where the routine stops; every interpretive word that can follow a `DANZIG` is a `CHANG2` resume key of its own. Each routine runs under the EBANK= the listing has in effect at its first word (E6 for the AOG code, E7 for the RTINIT/DVCNTR code). Under E6, 60GENRET (E6,1773) holds one of the two returns S61.1 saves there (TC 2224 from P61, TC 2321 from P62). Interpretive routines are entered at NEWOPS with FIXLOC a VAC area. A BOVB TCDANZIG (on overflow) runs ENDT(X)'s TC DANZIG natively (cm_integration_tcdanzig) and the routine goes on with the pair's right-hand order, as in flight (#414). The repository copy has a stray SKIP line before page 794 that assembles to nothing; the words are those of the flight rope.

## Evidence

Hashes: `evidence/cm-p61-p67/SHA256.json`; all files are in the tree.

- **Source map.** `cm-p61-p67-source-map.json` lists all 495 words of the file with the routine that compiles each (or why a constant is never run as code); 0 are untranslated.
- **Independent assembly comparison.** `make cm-p61-p67-differential` runs 256 states per routine (47,872 cases) through the original Comanche055 rope in yaAGC (pinned yaAGC with hardware DV and without the IMU/optics device block (tools/prepare_flight_device_engine.py), its ScalerCounter held at 0 (TIME1-TIME6 and the timer interrupts do not advance during a case)) and the native routines; all are equal in Z, 2048 erasable words (A, L 16-bit) and the interrupt-enable state. The cases and the stops reached per routine are in `cm-p61-p67-proof.json` (`cm-p61-p67-differential.log` is the run). One case in 128 is frozen in `tests/cm_p61_p67_vectors.h`.
- **Host and sanitizer results.** `make test-cm-p61-p67 sanitize-cm-p61-p67` runs the 374 frozen cases (`cm-p61-p67-host.log`).
- **C64 execution results.** `make vice-cm-p61-p67` runs 11 diagnostic programs with 251 cases (`cm-p61-p67-vice.log`, which starts with each PRG's SHA-256). The cases are chosen so the C64 runs take every control-flow edge (both outcomes of each branch) of every linked source that the 47,872 differential cases take: 2498 of 2498 edges and comparison outcomes (clang -O0 trace-pc-guard,trace-cmp) and 142 of 142 compiled labels. Checked again with gcov -b over gcc -O0 `--coverage` builds of the same sources: the C64 cases take 1357 of the 1357 branch outcomes all the cases take (`c64_gcov_branches` in the proof).
