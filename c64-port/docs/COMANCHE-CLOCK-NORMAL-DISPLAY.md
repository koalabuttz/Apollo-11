# Actual CLOCKJOB to normal noun40 display and interpreter restoration

`cm_clock_normal_display_step` consumes the genuine CLOCKJOB-selected
GOPLAY image and composes the qualified normal verb6/noun40 caller and
conversion services. It preserves the actual suspended Executive contexts.
The final phase calls canonical `cm_basic_finish` directly and records its
source-selected transfer; it does not require the idle-only outcome used by
the independent normal-display test factory.

Compiled phases7..28 follow `cm_normal_display_step`'s normal N40 consumer
from actual GOPLAY03312 through three mixed-noun rows and GOPLAY completion
at ENDOFJOB05217. Phase29 executes the actual END body through selected
basic DTCB, interpreted INTRSM before NEWOPS06037 or idle ADVAN as supported
by the qualified finish helper. Runtime image/IO and source-transfer metadata
are authoritative. This helper neither polls NEWJOB nor repeats an IRQ
admission or Executive exchange. Calls return before an overlay is replaced;
producer exclusion belongs to the caller. Unexpected source/service contexts
remain native fault boundaries with no transactional rollback promise.

The continuous proof begins at an actual suspended DANZIG06030 and executes
a real automatic T3 interrupt, SETJTAG/CLOKTASK/WAITLIST/NOVAC service path,
actual RESUME and pending DANZIG selection of CLOCKJOB24,3332/E6, actual
BANKCALL/QUICTRIG/SWRETURN, CKNVWRD1/REGODSP, MAKEPLAY, full canonical
PRIOCHNG and JOBWAKE, normal N40 consumer and actual ENDOFJOB. No original
image is replaced, normalized or filled with the separate display factory's
Pinball defaults. At genuine GOPLAY the source has A2/L0/Q3, source
COPINDEX2, core0 priority033110, NEWPRIO023000, NVWORD1=01450 and a saved
interpreter. Actual END restores that interpreter instead of becoming idle.

Nine unchanged source-normal mission contexts are selected from the prior
mission factory:0,2,3,6,7,10,11,14,15. They retain every word and external
field supplied by that factory. Other sampled contexts have MONSAVE1's
external-monitor bit020000 set; actual NVMONOPT returns via the blocked

The new helper has no private arithmetic or Executive implementation. It
