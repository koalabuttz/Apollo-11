# Translate Luminary VN1645's computation head

Lane E. Milestone `lm-vn1645`.

## Scope

From `Luminary099/P34-35_P74-75.agc`, VN1645's computation before its
display (bank 35 `03606`-`03634`, 23 words), P3XORP7X (`35,3704`-`3711`, 6
words) and the DP-.01 constant (`35,3741`-`3742`): 31 words on 31 source
lines. VN1645 saves QPRET in SUBEXIT and sets +MGA to -.01. If FINALFLG is
set it sets +MGA to -.02, and if REFSMFLG is also set it leaves the
interpreter, asks P3XORP7X whether a P3X or P7X program is running and, for
P3X, re-enters the interpreter to compute the middle gimbal angle of DELVSIN
through GET+MGA (the `lm-midgim` step, unchanged). Every path ends at GET45's
EXIT, at the basic frontier GET45 +1 (`35,3635`, TC COMPTGO). COMPTGO, the
DELAYJOB/GOFLASH display and the rest of VN1645 are executive and display
code that this milestone does not port.

`lm_vn1645(state, io)` returns one of these values:

- 1 at the basic frontier `35,3635`.
- 2 at the UNIT abort frontier (alarm 1302, from GET+MGA). No sampled entry
  reaches it.
- 3 at ARCSIN's argument-too-large frontier (`03723` in bank 0, alarm 1301).
- 0 with the image unchanged, when the entry is outside the contract.

Admitted entries must meet all of these conditions:

- The image is a canonical 2048-word image at NEWOPS of VN1645 (FBANK 35,
  LOC `03606`): EB and BB coherent, BANKSET = BB, INTBIT15 set, superbank
  bits clear, EDOP, ZERO and NEWJOB zero, every word 15 bits, MODE `+0`,
  `+1` or vector, and the VAC one of the five with PUSHLOC inside it.
- Any QPRET: VN1645 is entered by GOTO from P34/P35's display paths,
  SELECTMU and R30's caller.

The cases (`tests/lm_vn1645_cases.c`) give each path its share: FINALFLG
clear, REFSMFLG clear, P7X programs (74-77), P3X programs (34-37) and any
MODREG, with DELVSIN and a REFSMMAT rotation (sometimes with DELVSIN along
the stable-member Y axis). Raw edge words appear on one case in eight. All
of this sits over poisoned erasable debris with every VAC, MODE, OVFIND, IRQ
setting and allowed SUPERBNK value.

### How it is translated

The interpretive pairs are compiled with the general Luminary interpretive
bodies (`src/lm_interp_orders.c`) and the dispatch paths of
`src/lm_interp_steps.c`. P3XORP7X and the TC words around it are the
`agc_image_ops` instruction forms (CAF HIGH9, MASK MODREG, BZF, INCR Q,
RETURN). DP-.01 and HIGH9 appear as literals. Three steps sit between
original NEWOPS frontiers (`enum lm_vn1645_phase`, `lvn_*`): VN1645 through
P3XORP7X and TC INTPRET, the VLOAD PUSH DELVSIN / CALL GET+MGA pair, and
GET45's EXIT. GET+MGA keeps its `lm-midgim` phase number, and `lvn_remap`
follows its RVQ back to GET45.

### Shared fix: ARCSIN shift counts past 14

The VN1645 oracle found a mismatch in the shared `src/inverse_trig.c`.
ARCCOS's ACOSSHR un-normalizes the square root with `INDEX A; CAF BIT14`
(A = count-1). The source comment assumes counts below 15, but an argument
within about 2^-14 of +-1 with mixed-sign or third-word debris gives counts
of 15 to 28. The INDEX then reads past BIT1 into each mission's fixed-fixed
constant pool (NEG0, ZERO, FIVE, SEVEN, ELEVEN, ...; the LM and CM pools
differ after BITS4&5). The native `rounded_shift` used `040000 >> count`,
so it multiplied by +0 instead of -0, 5, 7 or 11. `shift_bit()` now
reproduces the pool for counts up to 28. The VN1645 stream reaches counts
15, 16 and 19. A targeted near-unity ARCSIN/ARCCOS run against yaAGC also
matches for counts 15, 16, 17 and 19 on both missions. The existing
inverse-trig streams (`make -f tools/inverse_trig.mk test check-host`) and
the `lm-midgim` stream are byte-identical.

## Evidence

Hashes are in `evidence/lm-vn1645/SHA256.json`. Bulk files are assets of
the `evidence-lm-vn1645` release (`python3 tools/evidence.py fetch
lm-vn1645`).

- **Source-to-native map and independent assembly comparison.**
  `python3 tools/lm_vn1645_source.py` reassembles the unchanged file with
  the pinned yaYUL. The rope equals the certified fixed-data rope. All 31
  words map to their native owners.
- **Oracle.** `tools/lm_vn1645_reference.c` (GPL, host only) runs the
  unchanged Luminary099 in the pinned yaAGC hardware-DV variant over 65536
  sampled entries. It stops at `35,3635`, the abort frontier or ARCSIN's
  alarm frontier. `tools/lm_vn1645_differential.py` checks the native
  producer against every entry and pins the stream identity
  (`lm-vn1645-source-stream.json`, SHA256 `1aba483e...31ab60`,
  269,090,816 bytes).
  - 63726 entries reach `35,3635` and 1810 reach the ARCSIN 1301 frontier.
  - Every path is taken: FINALFLG clear, REFSMFLG clear, P7X, P3X with
    GET+MGA's BPL both ways and the alarm.
- **Host and sanitizer results.** Run
  `make test-lm-vn1645 sanitize-lm-vn1645`. The strict build and the
  ASAN/UBSAN build each pass 14 frozen entries, 25 admission guards, 41
  frozen phase steps and the full 65536-entry stream
  (`lm-vn1645-checks.log`).
- **Phase frontiers.** `tools/freeze_lm_vn1645_phases.py` requires each of
  the 55 native frontiers of the 14 frozen entries to equal, in order, an
  original NEWOPS image of the oracle. It then freezes 41 steps in two
  groups.
- **C64 execution results.** Run `make vice-lm-vn1645`. Two PRGs (VN1645's
  steps; GET+MGA) keep both images in the RAM under KERNAL ROM (`$01=$35`).
  In VICE (PAL, stock ROMs) they pass 33 and 8 steps with no failures
  (`lm-vn1645-results.json`).

## Limits

- VN1645 stops at GET45 +1. COMPTGO, the V16N45 display and its responses
  are not translated, and the rest of P34-35_P74-75 is not covered by this
  milestone.
- Numerical validity is the original's. No wider claim is made.
