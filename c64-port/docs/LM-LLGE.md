# Translate Luminary LUNAR_LANDING_GUIDANCE_EQUATIONS

Lane F (LM powered flight and DAP). Milestone `lm-llge`. How the routines are made and checked: `docs/INTERP-COMPILE.md`.

`Luminary099/LUNAR_LANDING_GUIDANCE_EQUATIONS.agc` (`src/lm_llge.c`, generated by `tools/interp_compile.py lm-llge`) is native C on the control-transfer ABI, every word of it. The file is the landing guidance of P63 to P67: the guidance pass the SERVICER reaches through AVEGEXIT (LUNLAND, ?GUIDSUB and GUIDSUB with the phase tables NEWPHASE to WHATALM), the P64 redesignation (REDESIG, REDESMON and its counter in PITFALL's bank), the time-to-go and target logic (TTFINCR, TTF/8CL with the ROOTPSRS polynomial root finder, QUADGUID, CGCALC and the EXTLOGIC phase switch), the P65 to P67 starts and the rate-of-descent task (STARTP66, STARTP67, RODTASK, RODCOMP, VHORCOMP), the guidance displays (P63DISPS, P64DISPS, VERTDISP, TDISPSET) and FASTCHNG.

249 routines start at the entries (LUNLAND, ?GUIDSUB, GUILDEN, PITFALL, DESCBITS, P63DISPS -2, INTPRETX, ROOTPSRS, FASTCHNG, RODTASK, STARTP66, STARTP67, P65START, STARTP64, REDESMON, and DISPEXIT +3, where VHORCOMP in bank 32 returns through TC BANKCALL), at every point another file returns to, at the CADR words after `TC USPRCADR` (ROOTPSRS +22, ROOTLOOP +16 and +23, `usprcadr_exits`: USPRCADR runs the routine the CADR names with EXIT pending in EDOP, and its DANZIG continues at the CADR word, so each is a pending-DANZIG routine that exits to the next word), at words nothing in the file reaches (`resume_orphans`), and at every word after a DANZIG (`resume_keys`): the guidance runs as a job, so a DANZIG can find a job waiting and stop at CHANG2, and the Executive resumes the job there. `RTB LOADTIME` is native (`src/rtb_angles.c`), and `RTB NORMUNIT` is too (`src/lm_normunit.c`, the NORM front of `src/rtb_vector.c` and UNIT's phases of `src/rtb_unit.c`).

The channel orders are native on the shared channel model (`include/agc_channel_ops.h`, `'channels': 'all'`, `-DIRC_INPUTS -DIRC_CHANNELS -DIRC_LM_HW`): `READ CHAN30` (STERN, in GUILDEN) and `RAND`/`READ CHAN31` (the mode-control and hand-controller checks in STABL?, RATESTOP, PITFALL, REDESMON and its counting loop) read the latched inputs of the run, and `WOR CHAN13` with its `EXTEND` in STARTP64 (31,2635) and in REDESMON's RESETRPT (11,2336) writes the program's `agc_hardware` channel 13. The differential draws the inputs per case and seeds channels 13, 30 and 31 on both sides (`io_channels`), hands yaAGC the same values, and compares channel 13 and the trap flags after the run.

PITFALL (RUPT10) and RODTASK (a WAITLIST task) run in interrupt context, so the spec sets `any_fixloc` and `any_newjob`. Those apply to the whole file: every basic routine that never names FIXLOC (46 of the 84, job routines such as LUNLAND, STARTP66 and FASTCHNG among them) takes any FIXLOC and NEWJOB -0 and checks its entry with `sane_nv`. In flight builds the entry checks skip their whole-image scan (`trusted_image`).

## Pinned erasables

The cases hold these erasables to the values flight gives them (`all_states`, `range_states`):

- WCHPHASE and WCHPHOLD: a phase, -1 (IGNALG) to 2.
- WCHVERT: +10 (STARTP67's `CAF TEN`), 2, 1, +0 (P66), -1 and -2 (P65START's `CS TWO`); it is also NGUIDSUB, the guidance passes left.
- NIGNLOOP: 0 to 40 (IGNALG sets 40 and DDUMCALC in THE_LUNAR_LANDING counts it down).
- X1: -TARGTDEX, as INTPRETX (its only writer) sets it.
- PUSHLOC: vac+0 to vac+036 (even), the file's own pushdown below 32D, so a push never reaches X1. SERVOUT and FINDVAC give vac+0, and the file pushes only DP and vector words.
- At the resume points, the MODE and PUSHLOC the orders before them leave: vector MODE before the NORMUNITs at CGCALC +24, +26 and +29 and EXNORM +4; MODE 0 and an empty pushdown at QUADGUID +32; PUSHLOC vac+0 at TTF/8CL +26 and +28.
- ROOTPSRS, entered as TTF/8CL's TC leaves it: A = TABLTTFL, L = N-1 (1 or 2), Q = 31,3113 and WCHPHASE -1 to 1. Its loop points hold the table pointers it sets (`ROOTPS_STATES`).

DISPEXIT's `INDEX WCHPHOLD` / `TCF WHATDISP` with WCHPHOLD -1 lands on WHATEXIT's `TCF EXNORM`, which loops back to DISPEXIT for ever, so yaAGC never reaches a stop. Flight never gets there with WCHPHOLD -1. During IGNALG, P63 sets WCHPHASE -1 and AFCCLEND copies it to WCHPHOLD, and EXTLOGIC then goes to EXGSUB, never to EXBRAK and DISPEXIT. BURNBABY sets both to 0 at ignition. So cases entering CGCALC to EXGSUB hold WCHPHOLD 0 to 2, or (a third of them, the IGNALG pass) WCHPHOLD and WCHPHASE both -1. Cases entering EXBRAK to P63DISPS and 1406P00 to FASTCHNG, whose runs reach DISPEXIT, hold WCHPHOLD 0 to 2.

## Compiler changes

`tools/interp_compile.py` gained `index_tcf`, `basic_fixed_index`, `index_erasable`, `intpret_via`, `in_file_returns`, `returns` 0, `rtb` stop codes, `TC A`, `INCR BBANK` and `index_carry16` (listed in `docs/INTERP-COMPILE.md`). Every other spec still regenerates byte-identical (`--check`).

`index_carry16` fixes a C64-only fault in the generated `ixw()` (INDEX K): its end-around carry tested bit 16 of an `unsigned`, which is 16 bits on the 6502. There, `INDEX WCHPHASE` with WCHPHASE -1 lands one word low, and the indexed TCF table refuses. The host builds were right, so only the C64 diagnostic showed it. Other specs keep the old form until the steward decides on a shared regeneration.

## Evidence

Hashes: `evidence/lm-llge/SHA256.json`.

- **Source map.** `lm-llge-source-map.json` lists all 991 words with the routine that compiles each; none is left untranslated.
- **Independent assembly comparison.** `make lm-llge-differential`: 512 states for each of the 249 routines (127,488 cases, with the flight edge values of `tools/edge_cases.py` in a quarter; the erasables above are pinned (WCHPHASE and WCHPHOLD a phase, -1 to 2; WCHVERT +10, 2, 1, +0, -1 or -2; NIGNLOOP 0 to 40; X1 -TARGTDEX; PUSHLOC within the file's pushdown); channels 13, 30 and 31 are random) run the unmodified Luminary099 rope in yaAGC (with the hardware divide) to the same stop. All 127,488 equal it in Z, all 2,048 erasable words, the interrupt-enable state, channels 13, 30 and 31 and the trap flags. None is refused or left uncompared.
- **Host and sanitizer results.** `make test-lm-llge sanitize-lm-llge` run the 3,984 frozen cases (every 32nd).
- **C64 execution results.** `make vice-lm-llge` runs 49 diagnostics (routine groups that fit below $bf00, the image at $e000 under the KERNAL) with 380 cases chosen by `tools/c64_row_cover.py`. They take all 2,477 branch arcs (gcov, every linked source) the traced host cases (every 8th) take, and all 2,464 the 127,488 cases take when run in one process; the traced set is the larger because it also holds the truncated rows below (`lm-llge-c64-cover.json`). Arcs only long runs take come from truncated rows (`c64_cuts`, `tests/interp_cut.h`: the case runs to the first NEWOPS at a later routine, where yaAGC also stops and the digests are compared), 29 of the 380: the freeze cut 46 rows, and row selection kept the 29 that take an arc no whole run does. A few rare inputs whose arcs only long runs reached get their own states (`joint_states`): TREDES 0 at RED-OVER, and axis vectors (1/2 or 1/4, the other components ±0) for RTB NORMUNIT and VLOAD UNIT, where the unit divide leaves no remainder. The proof's own count, `c64_gcov_branches` in `lm-llge-proof.json` (the number `tools/cover_check.py` and `cert_selfcheck` check), is 2,434, all taken on C64 with 0 missing. It is smaller than 2,464 because it is `gcov -b -c` over the compiled sources only (`src/lm_llge.c` and the runtime it links): the row cover's 2,464 also counts the inline channel orders in `include/agc_channel_ops.h`, the runner driver `tools/interp_routine_native.c`, and the runner's own builds of the interpreter orders, and keys each arc by line.
