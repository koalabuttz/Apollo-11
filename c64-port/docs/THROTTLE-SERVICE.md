# P66 force feedback and throttle service

`p66_throttle_feedback_compute` implements the original P66 measured-acceleration feedback and force clamps. It enters at DANZIG with LOC03433, MODE077776, the measured DELVROD vector in MPAC, and PUSHLOC at the owned VAC base. Stable inputs are VBIAS, MASS, LAG/TAU, MAXFORCE and MINFORCE. It updates LASTTPIP and the desired acceleration AFC, and leaves the measured scalar acceleration in MPAC. The exit is DANZIG LOC03507, EDOP0, MODE0, PUSHLOC and ADDRWD at the VAC base. Division overflow continues through the historical source sequence; the documented square-root alarm boundary remains explicit.

`p66_throttle_service` implements the basic THROTTLE+3 routine from PC02221 in bank31 through THDUMP PC02367. Its entry A/L is the measured acceleration obtained by the caller's original EXIT/DXCH-MPAC bridge. AFC supplies the desired acceleration. The module converts both accelerations through MASSMULT, retains source saturation and critical-force decisions, calls native FASTCHNG, writes PIF/THRUST/channel14, and computes both historical FWEIGHT terms with native hardware-DV arithmetic. No decoder, emulator, floating point or heap participates in the native computation.

The service requires BB062007, a saved12-bit caller Q, one of the ordinary channel7 SUPER words000,020,040,060, and an interrupt-enable flag0 or1. Any ordinary MODE is retained. Unsupported caller control state rejects without changing any object. The caller owns distinct ordinary15-bit words, source scratch aliases, and producer exclusion; A/L are full16-bit registers. The service stops before THDUMP's saved-return dispatch. The enclosing driver owns EXIT/BANKCALL and interpreter reentry.

The additional service state maps FCODD to03465/6, FP03467/70, PIF03612/13, FC03615, FCOLD03620, PIFPSET03606, PSEUDO5503614, TTHROT03617, RTNHOLD03607, THRUST0055 and channel14. LOWCRIT/HIGHCRIT map02506/7, MODREG01011, and phase3_address01442/43. Q/BB/SUPER/interrupt state are explicit. Existing scratch BUF0130/1, MPAC0154..0162, ADDRWD0116 and MPTEMP0135 retain their original alias effects.

The feedback proof compares16,384 complete124-word source states, including10,083 minimum clamps,3,544 maximum clamps and2,757 unclamped paths. Snapshot SHA256: `a21344afc9e88cbe1adeec50b2c0b7645458b45b963ac0712d583bdff4d26d57`. Strict host and complete-state sanitizer checks pass. A PAL C64 in VICE passes64 frozen complete states, mailbox `a500004000`, PRG42,742 bytes, RAM end `$afef`, zero extra zero-page storage,44 hardware-stack bytes including ROM and328 software-stack bytes.

The service proof compares16,384 complete147-word states against the unchanged historical instruction/data rows and pinned hardware-DV engine. Fixture comment revisions are explicitly ignored in the source-row equality check. Corpus covers both zero signs, mixed limbs, full16-bit register overflow inputs, all critical-force branches, both guidance periods and interval fractions above unity. The original source visits FP compensation in2,880 cases, flatout with unchanged force in4,287, throttle-up in4,770, weighting in2,049, force saturation in9,634 and nonsaturating conversion in8,117 cases. Sixteen pulse-command overflow cases skip the original TS THRUST; the retained old THRUST is compared too. Snapshot SHA256: `4e2d490f0256e4ddb9110e42ad2278ae74acfe06164ac6bd4b17c361fafff37a`.
