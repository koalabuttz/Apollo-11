# Translate P51-P53

Lane D1b (alignment and maneuvers). Milestone `p51-p53`. How the routines are made and checked: `docs/INTERP-COMPILE.md`.

`Luminary099/P51-P53.agc` (`src/lm_p51_p53.c`) and `Comanche055/P51-P53.agc` (`src/cm_p51_p53.c`) are native C, every word. These are the IMU alignment programs:

- P51, IMU orientation determination.
- P52, IMU realignment.
- P53 and P54, the backup alignments (Comanche).
- P57, the lunar-surface alignment (Luminary).
- The star-sighting and mark routines R51, R52, R53, R55 and R56, and the LM's R59.
- S50 (LOCSAM), the planet and star geometry.
- The star-pair search PICAPAR over the 37-star catalog.

## Scope

| Program | Banks | Words | Routines |
|---|---|---|---|
| Luminary099 | 04 (3013-3026), 14 (2446-3773), 15 (2050-3663) | 1,646 | 696 |
| Comanche055 | 11, 13, 14, 15, 16, 17, 26 | 1,416 | 585 |

Entries:

- Luminary: P51, P57, R51, R52, R55 and R59.
- Comanche: P51, R51, R52, R53, R55, R56, S50, ADVORB (which P20-P25 CALLs), TERM52 (SXTMARK POSTJUMPs there) and R53C1 (R53's BANKCALL).

P52, P53, P54 and the option routines are reached through P51's and P57's option tables (`INDEX` + `TCF` jump tables, spec `tc_tables`).

The basic routines the interpretive code reaches through RTB run natively in `src/p51_p53_rtb.c`: RDCDUS (the CDU read into the VAC area) and SET1/PDT. V1STO2S, 1STO2S and LOADTIME use `src/rtb_angles.c`, and SGNAGREE uses `src/rtb_agreement.c`.

Destinations outside the file (the display interface, the Executive, BANKCALL and BANKJUMP, the restart and flag routines, the other alignment files) stop with `EXEC_UNMAPPED` until their files have routines. SWCALL, SWRETURN and DANZIG do not return to the file.

Compiler additions this file needed (`tools/interp_compile.py`):

- Indexed `TCF` tables, and `INDEX Q; TC n`.
- `fixed_index` offsets count from the operand as written: XUNIT's `-6,2` with X2 at -6, -4 or -2 is `{'XUNIT': [2, 4, 6]}`.
- An `SLOAD*` into fixed memory without a `fixed_index` entry is now a compile error, since its indexed path reads erasable. MKDNCDR's table was read from erasable before this.
- The CATLOG star table is read through `indexed_fixed`. Comanche's last catalog words come from the rope binary (`rope_bin`), because the listing leaves them out.

## Evidence

Hashes: `evidence/p51-p53/SHA256.json`. Bulk files are assets of the `evidence-p51-p53` release.

- **Source map.** `p51-p53-source-map.json` lists every emitted word with the routine that compiles it. Luminary has 1,646 words and Comanche 1,416, none untranslated.
- **Independent assembly comparison.** `make p51-p53-differential` runs 512 states per routine and program: 655,872 cases. They equal yaAGC in Z, in all 2,048 erasable words and in the interrupt-enable state. A quarter of the cases put edge values into MPAC and every operand. TIME1, TIME2 and the CDUs vary in every case. The cases cover:
  - BESTI and BESTJ across the 37 catalog stars and the three planet codes;
  - STARCODE and POSCODE (Luminary);
  - MARKSTAT (Comanche).
- **Index registers and resume points.** `index_states` gives each routine X1 or X2 as base minus a target in switched erasable when its next indexed operand names erasable, as the AXC and AXT settings in the file do. Per-point states pin MODE, PUSHLOC, X1, X2, S1 and S2 inside loops and at mid-sequence resume points:
  - the PICAPAR pair loop;
  - CALOOP, S52.2A, GRAVEL and WRTDESIR (Luminary);
  - CALCSMSC and ADVTRACK (Comanche).

  A DANZIG that finds NEWJOB positive stops at CHANG2, and every word after a DANZIG is a routine of its own (`resume_keys`). Runs may take up to 10,000 routines (`max_steps`): the star-pair search tries up to 666 pairs.
- **Waitlist task (Luminary).** GRABGRAV (15,2731) and its IBNKCALL and FINDVAC returns run as a TWIDDLE waitlist task, from whatever FIXLOC and NEWJOB the interrupted job or DUMMYJOB leaves. With `any_fixloc` and `any_newjob` (Luminary only: Comanche's basic routines here run in the programs' own jobs), Luminary's basic routines that never name FIXLOC or NEWJOB and never enter the interpreter take any FIXLOC and NEWJOB -0, and half and a quarter of their cases set them.
- **Host and sanitizer results.** `make test-p51-p53 sanitize-p51-p53` runs the 3,730 frozen cases: every 192nd case, plus a greedy cover of every branch outcome any case reaches.
- **C64 execution results.** `make vice-p51-p53` runs 66 diagnostics with 1,565 cases. They are chosen so that all 6,604 branch outcomes (and all 5,692 gcov branch arcs, `p51-p53-c64-cover.json`) the host cases reach are reached on the C64 too (`p51-p53-c64-outcomes.json`). The log records each PRG's sha256.
