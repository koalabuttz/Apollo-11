# Original UNIT and complete NORM numerical callers

`rtb_unit_begin` implements the original normal UNIT prologue, executing TC VECAGREE once before returning at UNIT+1. `rtb_unit_phase` then runs the three source arithmetic phases after VSQSUB, normalized SQRTSUB and final unit-vector division. Both missions retain all canonical scratch and the complete owned VAC. The same phases compose with the frozen NORMUNIT/NORMUNX1 frontiers to reach the actual mission DANZIG. This closes their numerical callee, while RTB closure still requires the gyro hardware service and CM QUALITY groups.

UNIT+1 deliberately skips VECAGREE: after NORM’s minor rescaling, a major negative zero may remain. The phase copies those exact raw pairs into VBUF, saves and clears OVFIND, then reuses the immutable unrounded ABVAL square helper with the original UNIT DOTRET03031. It therefore preserves source signed zeros rather than invoking UNIT’s initial sign-agreement step again. Root and division use the frozen UNIT phases; their original arithmetic dependencies and byte identities are retained in the proof. Each phase gathers only the actual scratch aliases and complete VAC, then commits everything before return. No pointer/private continuation survives a call and no Executive poll is added inside a basic body.

The input contract is nonnull canonical image/io, ordinary 15-bit memory with raw 16-bit A/L, coherent bank0 context, vector MODE−1, and one of the five original valid VAC bases. Phase0 ends PC03031/Q0140; phase1 ends PC03043/Q3; phase2 ends DANZIG06061 LM or06030 CM/Q3. Division overflow reaches original DANZIG/Q02413. An exposed 1302 path stops before SQRTABRT03402/Q03043, but the retained constructor corpus reaches no such abort checkpoint and claims no synthetic abort qualification. Pending LOC/EDOP, PUSHLOC, banks and external IRQ/SUPER are retained. Original caller producers remain excluded across all phases.

All 1,179,648 complete original-machine, strict-native and full-ASAN-UBSAN checkpoints match. Each of the two missions × three callers (NORMUNIT, NORMUNX1, normal UNIT) × three phases has 65,536 cases. Expected states come from the unchanged original caller entry through the actual source subroutine boundary, independently of native prefix computation. Tiny/minor-only and signed-zero cohorts cover the NORM prescale route and normal UNIT underflow/zero overflow; varied ordinary vectors include normal and source division-overflow paths. Coherent EB/SUPER, five VAC bases, old VAC values, raw A/L and fixed nonzero unowned-memory contexts vary. The constructor corpus is a joint sample, not exhaustive Cartesian multiword coverage.

Eighteen actual C64 PRGs pass 576 original-only complete frozen states. Retained maps, mailboxes and measured stacks prove these standalone diagnostics, with zero compiler zero-page allocation and all sections below $C000. Some full-prefix PRGs exceed a single16KB overlay; these diagnostics do not claim resident flight application or live disk-overlay allocation. Public arithmetic phases are available for the separately qualified resident caller to bind without introducing extra scheduling checks.

The original UNIT/UNITDV plus VSQSUB group maps125 source words per mission against the unchanged original declaration ropes, with binary bank0..3 ordering explicitly handled. Original source/support hashes and disclosed CM indentation repairs follow the immutable POWERED audits. This is a complete UNIT numerical group qualification within the stated vector/VAC caller contract, not a whole INTERPRETER source-file certificate. Independent read-only source/native review found no defect.
