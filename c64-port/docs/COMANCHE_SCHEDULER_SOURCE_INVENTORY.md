# Original Comanche Executive and Waitlist inventory

`evidence/cm-scheduler-source-audit.json` maps every original source line in
`EXECUTIVE.agc` and `WAITLIST.agc` to its independently assembled original-layout
address and words, source classification, native service group and bounded
proof. The authenticated rope is `6e3150ae...`. The historical files remain
unchanged. The inventory covers 337 assembled Executive words and 252 Waitlist
words, including immutable words and assembler directives.

This is an inventory, not a complete-file qualification certificate. The
reported `complete_file_qualified` remains false. Positive/dormant/-0 core and
ordinary compiled caller domains have retained source comparisons, including
basic/interpreter exchanges and genuine physical transfers. The original
CCSHOLE/+0 priority fault path can execute the numeric NO.CORES word as TC6;
that exceptional continuation and eventual alarm/restart bodies remain open.
Raw/high Q or indexed caller closure, every selected application/task body,
arbitrary interrupt timing and state combinations outside each proof also
remain explicit open domains. Named native helpers alone do not close them.

Regenerate with `python3 tools/cm_scheduler_source_audit.py` after
`make prepare-cm-original-rope`. Published bounded proof identities are listed
individually. New application translations and broader caller-domain proofs
must close the recorded gaps before changing the whole-file qualification.
