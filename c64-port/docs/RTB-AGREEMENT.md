# RTB sign agreement and fixed transpose source groups

`rtb_agreement` implements SGNAGREE and SIGNMPAC for both missions, plus Comanche TRANSP1 and TRANSP2. This packet qualifies 12 additional Luminary and 38 additional Comanche containing words, including the two XNB matrix ECADR constants. It supplements the immutable angle/time/PIPA packet. NORMUNIT/NORMUNX1, VECSGNAG, PULSEIMU and CM QUALITY remain outside these packets; neither complete source file is claimed.

SGNAGREE follows the original triple-precision carry sequence, clears all three limbs when they are all signed zeros, and preserves the final nonoverflow store into the minor limb. The literal helper return is through L, leaving Q equal to 2 and A equal to the source net sign indicator. SIGNMPAC tests the raw major sign, including negative zero, and retains the branch-specific old-pair L exchange. CM transposes swap three fixed double-precision off-diagonal pairs, preserve source ECADR/A and normalized L, and update EB/BB to E5. Every entry stops before the original DANZIG entry and retains pending interpreted LOC/EDOP; it executes no following instruction and introduces no scheduling check inside a basic body.

All 393,216 complete original-assembly, strict-native and full ASAN/UBSAN states match across six mission/entry contexts. Each comparison includes all 2048 canonical words plus IRQ and SUPER. Each context has 65,536 cases: a separate 4096-case corner cohort covers signed zeros, mixed-sign limbs and extrema; the upper cohort retains a full 32,768-word sweep for every ordinary major/minor/vector or transpose input using distinct odd coefficients. Raw A/L overflow signs, all coherent EB and SUPER settings, varied ordinary MODE and two fixed nonzero unowned-memory patterns are included. This is a varied joint constructor corpus, not a Cartesian exhaustion of all multiword operands.
